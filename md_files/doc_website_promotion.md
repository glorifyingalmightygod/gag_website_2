# Website Promotion — Action Plan
_Glorifying Almighty GOD (GAG)_

## Strategy Overview

The website has two natural advantages:
1. **Deep content** — 27 OT book survey pages with structured, detailed text — ideal for search engines
2. **Existing social presence** — YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp already set up

The promotion plan works on three tracks simultaneously:
- **SEO** — Let Google bring visitors passively over time
- **Social Media** — Drive traffic from existing channels consistently
- **Podcast Directories** — Reach a new audience through audio

## Track 1 — Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

> **Goal:** Get Google to discover and rank the website so people searching for Bible study content find it organically.
> **Effort:** Low (mostly one-time setup) | **Timeline:** 3–6 months to see results | **Cost:** Free

### Action Items

- [ ] **Create a `sitemap.xml` file** in the root folder listing all 66 book pages, doctrine pages, and special pages — helps Google find every page quickly

- [ ] **Register on Google Search Console**
  - URL: https://search.google.com/search-console
  - Sign in with glorifyingalmightygod@gmail.com
  - Add your website property (domain or URL prefix)
  - Submit your sitemap.xml
  - Monitor which search terms bring visitors over time

- [ ] **Register on Bing Webmaster Tools** (secondary, but still useful)
  - URL: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
  - Submit the same sitemap.xml

- [ ] **Add meta description tags** — Each book page ideally has a unique description (e.g. "A complete survey of the Book of Daniel — 12 chapters, key persons, prophecies, foreshadows of Christ"). Currently all pages share the same generic site description in `index.html`

- [ ] **Verify Google Analytics is receiving data** after the site goes live
  - URL: https://analytics.google.com (GA4 tag: `G-8VJL1JF1HW`)
  - Check Real-time reports when you visit the site yourself

- [ ] **Add the website URL to all your social media profiles** — Facebook Group description, Instagram bio, YouTube channel description — creates backlinks that improve search ranking

## Track 3 — Podcast Directories

> **Goal:** List your audio sermons on major podcast platforms so a completely new audience can discover the ministry through their usual podcast apps.
> **Effort:** Low (one-time setup per platform) | **Timeline:** 1–2 weeks to get approved | **Cost:** Free

### How Podcast Submission Works

Podcast directories require an **RSS feed** — a special URL that lists all your episodes with metadata. You need to either:
- Use a free podcast hosting service (they generate the RSS feed automatically), OR
- Create the RSS feed manually (more technical)

**Recommended approach: Use a free podcast host**

### Recommended Free Podcast Hosts

| Service | Free Plan | Direct Link |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Anchor (Spotify for Podcasters)** | Unlimited episodes, free forever | podcasters.spotify.com |
| **Buzzsprout** | 2 hours/month free | buzzsprout.com |
| **Podbean** | 5 hours/month free | podbean.com |

> **Recommended: Spotify for Podcasters (Anchor)** — completely free, no episode limit, and automatically distributes to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and Google Podcasts from one place.

## Notes

- All three tracks (SEO, Social, Podcast) work together — a visitor who finds the site via Google might follow on Instagram; a podcast listener might join the WhatsApp group

- The OT book survey pages are genuinely unique and detailed content — there is very little free content this thorough for each Bible book. That is your competitive advantage in search
